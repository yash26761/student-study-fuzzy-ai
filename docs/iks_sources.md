# Verified Sources for IKS Connection

This document records the verified, official sources supporting the Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) connection for the project:
**"AI-Based Student Study Time Recommendation System Using Fuzzy Logic"**.

---

## Source 1: National Education Policy 2020 (NEP 2020)

- **Source Title:** National Education Policy 2020
- **Organization:** Ministry of Education (formerly Ministry of Human Resource Development), Government of India
- **Publication Year:** 2020
- **Official URL:** [https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/NEP_Final_English_0.pdf](https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/NEP_Final_English_0.pdf)
- **Relevant Sections:**
  - **Preamble & Fundamental Principles (p. 4–5):** Emphasizes holistic and multidisciplinary education, recognizing the unique capabilities of each student, promoting individual pace of learning, and moving away from rigid, one-size-fits-all rote examination pressure.
  - **Section 4.27 (p. 18):** Directs the inclusion and integration of Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS), indigenous pedagogical wisdom, and philosophical foundations of learning into contemporary educational systems.
  - **Section 11 (p. 36–37):** Encourages holistic, broad-based education (*Samagra Shiksha*) that balances intellectual, mental, and physical well-being.
- **What This Source Supports:**
  - Validates that the Indian educational perspective is learner-centered, holistic, and emphasizes individual learning readiness and student well-being over rigid, uniform pressures.
  - Establishes official Government of India support for applying IKS concepts to modernize student learning and academic guidance.
- **Where Used in Project:**
  - `README.md` (IKS Connection section)
  - `docs/iks_connection.md` (Sections 1, 2, 3, 8, 10)
  - `docs/viva_notes.md` (IKS Viva Questions 3, 4, 10)
  - `app.py` (Educational perspective expander)

## Source 2: Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) Division — Ministry of Education

- **Source Title:** Mandate, Objectives and Guidelines of the IKS Division
- **Organization:** IKS Division, Ministry of Education, Government of India (Hosted at AICTE, New Delhi)
- **Establishment Year:** October 2020
- **Official URL:** [https://iksindia.org/](https://iksindia.org/)
- **Relevant Focus Areas:**
  - Promotion of interdisciplinary research bridging traditional Indian knowledge traditions with modern science, technology, and computing.
  - Revitalization of learner-centric, self-directed learning traditions (*svadhyaya* — self-study and self-reflection) and balanced effort (*yukta abhyasa*) to solve contemporary problems in student wellness and education.
- **What This Source Supports:**
  - Confirms the academic validity of connecting traditional Indian educational perspectives with modern computer science, AI, and decision-support systems.
  - Supports the view that IKS provides the human-centered, philosophical framing for technology rather than obsolete computational dogma.
- **Where Used in Project:**
  - `README.md` (IKS Connection section)
  - `docs/iks_connection.md` (Sections 1, 4, 8, 10)
  - `docs/viva_notes.md` (IKS Viva Questions 1, 4, 6)

## Source 3: UGC Guidelines on Incorporating IKS into Higher Education Curricula

- **Source Title:** Guidelines for Training/Orientation of Faculty on Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS)
- **Organization:** University Grants Commission (UGC), Ministry of Education, Government of India
- **Publication Year:** 2023
- **Official URL:** [https://www.ugc.gov.in/](https://www.ugc.gov.in/)
- **Relevant Focus Areas:**
  - Highlights traditional Indian pedagogical methods where mentorship and learning schedules are tailored to the *adhikara* (student's individual capacity, readiness, and present context).
  - Emphasizes harmony, mental calm, and balanced daily routine (*dinacharya*) to avoid academic burnout.
- **What This Source Supports:**
  - Supports the project's foundational premise: academic guidance must be customized to the student's individual reality (time left, hours already put in, current confidence) rather than enforcing rigid, identical targets for everyone.
- **Where Used in Project:**
  - `docs/iks_connection.md` (Sections 3, 4, 10)
  - `docs/viva_notes.md` (IKS Viva Questions 3, 5, 8)

## Source 4: Classical Foundation of Fuzzy Logic (Non-IKS Baseline)

- **Source Title:** "Fuzzy Sets", *Information and Control*, Vol. 8, Issue 3, pp. 338–353
- **Author:** Lotfi A. Zadeh
- **Publication Year:** 1965
- **Official DOI:** [10.1016/S0019-9958(65)90241-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0019-9958(65)90241-X)
- **What This Source Supports:**
  - Establishes the factual, modern mathematical origin of Fuzzy Logic.
  - Used explicitly as a negative control / limitation baseline to verify and defend that fuzzy sets were formulated mathematically in 1965 by Lotfi Zadeh, preventing any false or unscientific claims that ancient Indian texts invented fuzzy logic algorithms.
